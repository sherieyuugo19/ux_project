## The idea in one sentence
SPOILER helps two people who read the same book at different times discuss it together without spoilers when they reach an agreed point in the story.

## Concept
SPOILER is a private reading space for two people who want to share the experience of reading the same fiction book. Each person reads independently and at their own pace, while saving private thoughts and reactions in the app. These reflections remain hidden until both readers are ready to reveal their thoughts for the same reading checkpoint.

The idea is to let both people form their own opinions before seeing the other person's perspective. The mutual reveal mechanic is a hypothesis to validate through user research.

## Research
The research will explore how people currently share reactions about books, how they avoid spoilers, how they coordinate reading progress, and whether they would find it useful to reveal their thoughts only when both readers are ready.

It will also investigate whether people prefer to use their existing book or reading app instead of having a built-in reader.
