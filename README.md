## The idea in one sentence
SPOILER helps two friends read the same fiction book independently, keep chapter-based reflections private, and reveal their thoughts together when both have finished the agreed reading checkpoint.

## Concept
SPOILER is a companion to the book or reading app people already use. Two friends choose the same book and agree on a chapter or other checkpoint. Each reads independently and saves private reflections in SPOILER. The reflections remain hidden until both readers submit; then they are revealed together.

The selected visual direction is **Warm Contemporary Editorial**, with a **Censored Spoiler** identity: redacted text, black bars and concealed fragments.

## Research
The new research will investigate both existing reading partners and friends who want to start reading together. It will validate whether readers want reflections to stay hidden until both have submitted, how they coordinate reading checkpoints, and what they currently do to avoid spoilers.

No findings from the previous DEEP CUT music-discovery concept are treated as evidence for SPOILER.
