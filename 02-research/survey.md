# DEEP CUT · Online Survey

## Research purpose

The purpose of this survey is to understand how people discover live music events, find concerts that match their musical preferences and explore local music venues.

The research focuses on two main areas:

1. **Personalised concert discovery:** How people find events featuring artists and music they already enjoy.
2. **Local music discovery:** How people discover unfamiliar venues, emerging artists and smaller local events.

## Research method

An online Google Forms questionnaire combining multiple-choice questions and open-ended written interview questions.

The questionnaire explores:
- Live music habits
- Concert discovery platforms and sources
- Music preferences and personalised recommendations
- Awareness of local music venues
- Difficulties and workarounds when searching for concerts
- Opportunities to improve the discovery experience

## Participants

Target group: People interested in live music and attending concerts.

Target number of responses: 3–5 participants for the written interview research.

## Data handling

Responses are collected anonymously and used for academic research. Personal information is not included in this public repository.
