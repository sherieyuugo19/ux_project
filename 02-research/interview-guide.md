# Interview guide · 15 to 20 minutes
Topic: how people <task>. I never mention my app idea during the interview.

1  Thanks for your time. I am studying how people <task>. There are no wrong answers.
2  May I take notes? I do not write down your name. You can stop at any time.
3  How does <task> usually fit into your week?
4  Tell me about the last time you <task>. Start from the moment you decided to do it.
5  What did you use? Can you show me?            (phone, paper, a person)
6  Where did it get slow, confusing or annoying?  then: why? what happened then?
7  What did you do to get around it?              (workarounds)
8  If it had gone perfectly, what would have been different?
9  Is there anything I should have asked and did not?
10 Thank you. May I come back with a prototype in a few weeks?

Probes: Why? · Tell me more. · What happened then? · Can you show me?
