# Interview guide · 15 to 20 minutes

Topic: how people discover and choose live music events that match their musical preferences and interests.

I never mention my app idea during the interview.

1. Thanks for your time. I am studying how people discover and choose live music events. There are no right or wrong answers.

2. May I take notes? I do not write down your name. You can stop at any time.

3. How does finding concerts and live music events usually fit into your week? Do you actively search for events or discover them by chance?

4. Tell me about the last time you searched for a concert or live music event. Start from the moment you decided to look for something.

5. What did you use to find the event? Can you show me? (phone, websites, apps, social media, recommendations from other people)

6. Where did the process get slow, confusing or annoying? Was there any information you couldn't find? Why? What happened next?

7. What did you do to get around the problem? Did you try another platform, search differently, ask someone or give up?

8. If the experience had gone perfectly, what would have been different? What would have made finding and choosing a concert easier?

9. Is there anything else about how you discover concerts, artists or local music venues that I should have asked?

10. Thank you. May I come back with a prototype in a few weeks?

Probes: Why? · Tell me more. · What happened then? · Can you show me?
