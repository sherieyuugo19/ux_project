# SPOILER · Interview guide · 15–20 minutes

## Research topic

How people read fiction, share reading experiences and avoid unwanted spoilers when reading the same book with another person.

## Opening and consent

Thank you for taking part. I am researching how people read fiction and share their thoughts about books.

There are no right or wrong answers. You can skip any question or stop at any time. I will use an anonymous participant code instead of your name.

May I take written notes during the interview?

## Questions

1. How often do you read fiction, and what format do you usually use?

2. Have you ever read the same book as another person? Tell me about the most recent experience.

3. How did you choose the book and decide how much to read?

4. Did you read at the same pace or separately? How did you know how far the other person had progressed?

5. How did you share thoughts or discuss the book? Were there any concerns about spoilers?

6. Can you describe a situation when one of you was ahead in the book? What happened, and how did you handle it?

7. What was inconvenient or frustrating about sharing the reading experience? Did you find a workaround?

## Optional follow-up prompts

- Can you give me a specific example?
- What happened next?
- Why did you choose that approach?
- How did you feel about it?
- What would you change about that experience?

## Closing

Is there anything else about reading or discussing books with another person that you would like to add?

Thank you for your time.

## Research ethics

Use participant codes (P1, P2, etc.). Do not publish names, contact details, private messages, face photos or recordings in the public repository.

## AI use

ChatGPT helped organise and shorten the interview guide. The student will conduct the interviews, document anonymised observations and analyse the findings.
