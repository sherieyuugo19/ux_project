# Step 1 · My app idea

## Working title
<DEEP CUT>

## The idea in one sentence
<DEEP CUT helps music fans to discover concerts and emerging artists that match their musical taste when exploring the local live music scene.>.

## Who it is for
- User group: <Young adults and music enthusiasts who enjoy attending live concerts, discovering new artists and exploring different music genres>
- Where I can find five of them: <Friends and acquaintances who regularly attend concerts.
University students interested in live music.
Visitors of local music venues and smaller concerts.>
- Their device and situation: <Iphone users at any time>

## The problem (my hypothesis)
I believe <live music fans> struggle with <to discover concerts and emerging artists that match their musical taste> when <looking for upcoming events>,
because <concert information is spread across different ticketing platforms, venue websites and social media, while smaller artists and independent music venues can be difficult to discover>.
I will know I am right if, in interviews, they 
- Describe using several platforms or social media accounts to find concerts.
- Say they have missed concerts they would have liked to attend.
- Find it difficult to discover smaller local events and independent music venues.
- Consider distance and travel time when deciding whether to attend a concert.>.

## Platform and why
<iOS mobile application> because <users can discover concerts, explore nearby events and check venue information while on the go. Location-based features make it easier to find events near them and check how far away venues are. Users will be able to set their music preferences manually by selecting genres and favourite artists, or optionally connect their Spotify or Apple Music account.>.

## Out of scope: what my app will NOT do
1. Music streaming: The app will not play music or replace Spotify or other music streaming services.
2. Social networking: The app will not include messaging, social profiles or features for finding concert companions.
3. Full travel planning and navigation: The app will show venue locations, distances and estimated travel times, but detailed directions will be handled by external navigation services.
