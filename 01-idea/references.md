## A. Competitors: products addressing related user needs (4)

### 1. **Fable** · <https://fable.co/>
- **What it does for my users:** A reading-focused social platform with book clubs and shared discussion.
- **What I take from it:** A clear connection between reading progress and discussion with other readers.
- **What I would avoid:** Turning a private two-person experience into a public social feed or a large community-first product.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-01.png` · capture a relevant book club or discussion screen.

### 2. **Bookclubs** · <https://bookclubs.com/>
- **What it does for my users:** Helps people organise book clubs and coordinate shared reading.
- **What I take from it:** Clear group coordination, book selection and reading-schedule concepts.
- **What I would avoid:** Bringing multi-person club management and extensive administration into a deliberately small two-person experience.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-02.png` · capture a relevant club, schedule or discussion screen.

### 3. **Goodreads** · <https://www.goodreads.com/>
- **What it does for my users:** Lets readers track books, share ratings and reviews, and discover what other readers think.
- **What I take from it:** Familiar book identity, reading status and lightweight ways to record reactions.
- **What I would avoid:** A broad catalogue, ratings and public reviews becoming more important than the shared chapter-by-chapter experience.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-03.png` · capture a book page or review screen.

### 4. **The StoryGraph** · <https://thestorygraph.com/>
- **What it does for my users:** Supports personal reading tracking, statistics and book discovery.
- **What I take from it:** A focused personal reading-progress experience and clear status indicators.
- **What I would avoid:** Making statistics, recommendations or individual tracking the main purpose of a two-person reading room.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-04.png` · capture a reading status or tracking screen.

## B. UI/UX patterns: one interaction that can inform the journey (3)

### 5. **Kindle** · Highlights and notes · <https://www.amazon.com/kindle-dbs/fd/kcp>
- **The pattern:** Saving passages, highlights and personal notes while reading.
- **Why it fits SPOILER:** It can inform a lightweight reflection composer. SPOILER should capture a reader's own reaction without trying to reproduce a full e-reader.
- **What to avoid:** Requiring users to import or reproduce the full reading experience.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-05.png` · capture a highlight or note interaction.

### 6. **Apple Notes** · Shared notes and collaboration · <https://www.icloud.com/notes>
- **The pattern:** A familiar way to create notes and collaborate on shared content.
- **Why it fits SPOILER:** It can inform clear note composition and collaboration cues. SPOILER differs by keeping each person's reflections private until the shared checkpoint is complete.
- **What to avoid:** Showing edits or private thoughts immediately when the concept requires a mutual reveal.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-06.png` · capture a relevant note or collaboration screen.

### 7. **WhatsApp** · Message delivery and read states · <https://www.whatsapp.com/>
- **The pattern:** Clear message status and communication between two people.
- **Why it fits SPOILER:** It can inform understandable waiting, submitted and ready states between partners.
- **What to avoid:** Real-time chat as the main interaction; spontaneous messages can expose spoilers.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-07.png` · capture a relevant message-status screen.

## C. Visual references: colour, type, imagery and tone (3)

### 8. **A24** · <https://a24films.com/>
- **What fits SPOILER:** Distinctive independent-publishing energy and confident visual identity.
- **What I take from it:** A recognisable editorial attitude and expressive art direction.
- **What I would avoid:** Letting cinematic imagery overpower the clarity and usability of a functional app.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-08.png` · capture a relevant brand or title treatment.

### 9. **The New Yorker** · <https://www.newyorker.com/>
- **What fits SPOILER:** A strong relationship between editorial typography, reading and visual storytelling.
- **What I take from it:** Typographic hierarchy, thoughtful spacing and text-led composition.
- **What I would avoid:** A traditional or overly formal magazine look.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-09.png` · capture a relevant editorial layout.

### 10. **It's Nice That** · <https://www.itsnicethat.com/>
- **What fits SPOILER:** Contemporary creative publishing, bold graphic compositions and playful visual communication.
- **What I take from it:** Expressive layouts, strong graphic accents and a contemporary editorial tone.
- **What I would avoid:** Making the interface feel like an art portfolio rather than a clear mobile product.
- **Screenshot:** `img/ref-10.png` · capture a relevant graphic or editorial layout.
