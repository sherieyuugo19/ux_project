# Step 1 · Ten references

Screenshots go into 01-idea/img/ and are named ref-01.png, ref-02.png ...

## A. Competitors: what people use today for the same task (4)
### 1. **Bandsintown** · <https://www.bandsintown.com/>
![](img/ref-01.png)
- What it does for my user: Helps users discover nearby concerts, follow favourite artists and receive personalised event recommendations.
- What I take from it: Music-based recommendations, nearby event discovery and clear concert information.
- What I would avoid: Making artist tracking and notifications the main focus instead of encouraging exploration of emerging artists and independent local music scenes.


### 2. **Songkick** · <https://www.songkick.com/>
![](img/ref-02.png)
- What it does for my user: Provides personalised concert listings, tracks favourite artists and recommends new bands based on music preferences.
- What I take from it: Personalised concert recommendations and the ability to follow favourite artists.
- What I would avoid: Making the experience too focused on tracking existing favourite artists rather than discovering emerging local talent.

### 3. **DICE** · <https://dice.fm/>
![](img/ref-03.png)
- What it does for my user: Helps users discover personalised live events, club nights and festivals, and purchase tickets.
- What I take from it: A personalised event feed, music preference integration and a clear event discovery experience.
- What I would avoid: Making ticket purchasing the main focus instead of music discovery.

### 4. **Resident Advisor (RA Guide)** · <https://ra.co/>
![](img/ref-04.png)
- What it does for my user: Helps users discover electronic music events, DJs, clubs and local nightlife.
- What I take from it: A strong connection to local music scenes and the discovery of niche artists and independent venues.
- What I would avoid: Limiting the experience to electronic music, since DEEP CUT aims to support different music genres.

## B. Patterns: one screen that solves one part of my task well (3)
### 5. **Resident Advisor** · <Event discovery> · <https://ra.co/>
![](img/ref-05.png)
- The pattern: Discovering local music events through an event feed with information about artists, dates and venues.
- Why it fits my app: It can inspire how DEEP CUT displays nearby concerts and helps users explore local music scenes.

### 6. **SoundCloud** · <Discover Feed> · <https://soundcloud.com/>
![](img/ref-06.png)
- The pattern: Discovering new music and artists through personalized recommendations based on listening habits and preferences.
- Why it fits my app: It can inspire how DEEP CUT introduces users to emerging artists who match their musical taste.


### 7. **DICE** · <Saved Events> · <https://dice.fm/>
![](img/ref-07.png)
- The pattern: Saving interesting events to revisit and consider attending later.
- Why it fits my app: It can inspire a saved events section where users can keep track of concerts they might want to attend.

## C. Visual references: colour, type, images, tone (3)
### 8. **Boiler Room** · <https://boilerroom.tv/>
![](img/ref-08.png)
- What fits my users: Raw concert photography, direct flash, grainy textures and underground club aesthetics create an authentic connection to independent music culture. I want to use this visual direction to give DEEP CUT an energetic and alternative atmosphere without making the interface too difficult to navigate.

### 9. **Spotify** · <https://open.spotify.com/>
![](img/ref-09.png)
- What fits my users: Bold typography, expressive colours and distinctive artwork make music discovery visually engaging. I want to take inspiration from its clear content structure while allowing artists on DEEP CUT to keep their own visual identities instead of forcing them into one template.

### 10. **A24** · <https://a24films.com/films/mid90s>
![](img/ref-10.png)
- What fits my users: Analog photography, film grain, skate culture and imperfect textures create a youthful and authentic visual language. I want to use these elements as inspiration for DEEP CUT's modern grunge aesthetic while keeping individual artist artwork and photography unrestricted.
